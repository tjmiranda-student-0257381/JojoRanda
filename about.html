Who I Am

Hello! I am Joseph Randa from Hanford, CA. I am fully bilingual and speak both English and Tagalog fluently.

I am a proud Freemason from Welcome Lodge No. 255 and affiliated with Widows Son No. 869. I am also an active member of the Widows Sons Masonic Riders Association Brazen Pillars chapter. Previously, I was a member of the Men of Gillead chapter, and a prior member and past president of the Men of Tyre chapter.
